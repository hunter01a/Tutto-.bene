Holidays at Tuttobene

December

Christmas

The most awaited month: artisan panettone, glowing windows, gnomes and advent calendars. In December, Tuttobene becomes a little Christmas village.

Christmas
Candles, wreaths and gift ideas: in December the shop becomes a Christmas market
Christmas
Trees of light glowing above the counter, for aperitivo under the stars
January 17

Saint Anthony's Day

The local tradition of Sant'Antonio, with blessed sweets and rituals renewed every year.

Sant'Antonio
Sant'Antonio blessed buns, glossy with sugar, fresh out of the oven
February

Carnival

Crispy cenci and schiacciata alla fiorentina: Carnival at Tuttobene smells of fried dough and icing sugar.

Carnival
Mountains of freshly fried cenci that vanish from the counter within hours
Carnival
Warm fritters and cenci on the red plate: Carnival smells of icing sugar
February 14

Valentine's Day

Chocolate hearts, red ribbons and themed sweets: the lovers' day, in the window and in the lab.

Valentine's
Engraved glasses and little gifts: the counter dresses in red for lovers
Springtime

Easter

Artisan colomba cakes and decorated eggs: Easter closes the circle of holidays with the great leavened classics.

Easter
Artisan colomba cakes with almonds and pearl sugar, slowly leavened in the lab
Easter
Dark chocolate eggs with whole salted hazelnuts, on display until Easter
